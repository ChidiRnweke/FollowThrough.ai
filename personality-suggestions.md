Here’s a quick summary of the principles to make a note-taking app feel **clean and calm, yet distinct** (avoiding both visual noise and default SaaS templates):

* **Warm Neutral Palette:** Replace sterile digital whites and cold grays with subtle paper-like neutrals (e.g., `#FBFBF9` background, `#1C1C1A` ink-colored text, warm soft borders).
* **High-Precision Typography:** Use a distinct font pairing—like a warm serif for document titles and a crisp monospace for metadata/dates—to establish character without visual clutter.
* **Layout De-Cluttering:** Shift away from heavy gray box borders (`border-gray-200`) and standard grid cards toward generous whitespace, soft structural divisions, and borderless contrast.
* **Refined Micro-Interactions:** Focus on subtle feedback like focus-mode dimming, sleek vertical accent lines for active states, or fading sidebars when typing.
* **Purposeful Micro-Copy:** Keep navigation ultra-clean while using quiet, thoughtful copy for empty states and task completions instead of mechanical system text.
* **Distinctive Iconography:** Ditch standard line icons for subtle dual-tone glyphs, tinted indicators, or custom iconography that feels integrated into the layout rather than slapped on top.
